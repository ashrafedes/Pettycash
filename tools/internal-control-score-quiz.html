What Are Internal Controls?

Internal controls are the processes, procedures, and policies that ensure the accuracy of financial reporting, prevent fraud, and protect company assets. They include segregation of duties, documentation requirements, approval workflows, regular reconciliations, access controls, and audit trails. Strong internal controls are essential for business sustainability, regulatory compliance, and stakeholder confidence. Weak controls can lead to fraud, errors, financial losses, and reputational damage.

Release Notes

New features, improvements, and fixes. Updated August 2026.

New August 2026

Marketing Website Launch

Launched the PettyCash marketing website with homepage, features, pricing, blog, free business tools hub, PDF tools, templates library, industry pages, comparison pages, and trust center. Added bilingual support (Arabic/English) with RTL throughout.

Improvement July 2026

Enhanced Approval Workflows

Multi-level approval routing now supports up to 5 approval levels with conditional rules based on amount thresholds. Approvers receive email notifications with one-click approve/reject.

New June 2026

Denomination Counting

Added denomination-level cash counting for reconciliation. Count notes and coins by denomination, and the system automatically calculates the total and flags any variance from the expected balance.

Fix June 2026

VAT Calculation Fix

Fixed an issue where VAT was calculated on the total including VAT instead of the net amount. All VAT calculations now correctly use the net amount as the base.

New May 2026

Multi-Fund Support

Added support for unlimited petty cash funds. Each fund has its own custodian, spending limits, and transaction history. Perfect for multi-location businesses.

New April 2026

Initial Launch

PettyCash launched with core features: petty cash fund management, transaction tracking, receipt upload, basic reporting, Arabic/English support, and user management.

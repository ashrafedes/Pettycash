# Stage 9 — Blog & Content Strategy

**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Status:** ✅ Complete (Awaiting Approval)

## Overview

This stage defines the content strategy for the PettyCash.site blog. It includes 7 content categories, keyword clusters per category, a prioritized backlog of 200 article titles ranked by estimated search volume and commercial intent, and a 12-month publishing calendar.

## Content Categories

1. **Petty Cash** — Fund management, reconciliation, policies, best practices
2. **Expense Management** — Tracking, approval workflows, reimbursement, automation
3. **Accounting** — Bookkeeping, journal entries, financial statements, year-end
4. **Finance** — Budgeting, forecasting, cash flow, financial analysis
5. **Internal Control** — Audit, fraud prevention, segregation of duties, compliance
6. **Saudi VAT & Compliance** — ZATCA e-invoicing, VAT returns, Saudi labor law, Saudization
7. **Construction Finance** — Project accounting, subcontractor management, progress billing

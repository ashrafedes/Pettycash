When to Choose Odoo

Odoo is the better choice if you need a full ERP system that covers CRM, HR, inventory, manufacturing, and accounting in one platform. If you already use or plan to use Odoo for your business operations, its built-in petty cash handling through the accounting module is adequate. Odoo is ideal for companies that want an all-in-one system and have the technical resources to configure and maintain it.

When to Choose PettyCash

PettyCash is the better choice if petty cash management is your primary need and you do not want the complexity of a full ERP. If you need denomination counting, multi-fund management, or ZATCA compliance out of the box, or if you want a tool that sets up in 2 minutes without installing modules or configuring a chart of accounts, PettyCash is the right fit. It does one thing exceptionally well instead of doing many things adequately.

FAQ

Does Odoo have a petty cash module?

Odoo handles petty cash through its general accounting module rather than a dedicated petty cash feature. It works but lacks petty-cash-specific tools like denomination counting.

Is Odoo free?

Odoo Community edition is free but limited. Odoo Enterprise starts at $24/user/month. PettyCash has a free plan with no per-user pricing.

When to Choose Zoho Expense

Zoho Expense is the better choice if you need a full expense management ecosystem integrated with other Zoho apps (Books, CRM, Payroll), require corporate card integration or mileage tracking, or have a large organization that benefits from the broader Zoho suite. It is a solid general-purpose expense tool.

When to Choose PettyCash

PettyCash is the better choice if petty cash management is your primary need, you operate in Saudi Arabia and need ZATCA compliance and native Arabic support, you want denomination-level cash counting, or you need a simple tool that sets up in minutes without a complex ecosystem. PettyCash is purpose-built for petty cash, not a general expense tool with petty cash bolted on.

FAQ

Which is better for petty cash specifically?

PettyCash is purpose-built for petty cash management with features like denomination counting and multi-fund support. Zoho Expense is a general expense tool that handles petty cash as one of many features.

Is Zoho Expense free?

Zoho Expense has a free plan for up to 5 users. PettyCash also has a free plan with no user limit for petty cash management.

Does Zoho Expense support Saudi VAT?

Zoho Expense supports multi-currency and tax rates but does not have Saudi-specific ZATCA compliance features. PettyCash is built for the Saudi market.

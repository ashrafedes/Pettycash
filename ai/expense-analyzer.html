What the AI Detects

•

Duplicate expenses: Same vendor, amount, and date — even with slight description variations.

•

Missing receipts: Transactions above a threshold without a linked receipt.

•

VAT errors: Incorrect VAT calculation (e.g., VAT on gross instead of net, wrong rate applied).

•

Fraud indicators: Same receipt number on different dates, round-amount patterns, weekend submissions, and vendor name mismatches.

•

Policy violations: Expenses exceeding limits, unauthorized categories, or missing approvals.

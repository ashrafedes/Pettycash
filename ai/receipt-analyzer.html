Yes, the AI is trained on both Arabic and English receipts, including mixed-language receipts common in Saudi Arabia.

          
Can it detect fake or altered receipts?
The AI flags suspicious patterns such as inconsistent fonts, altered amounts, and duplicate receipt numbers. Flagged receipts are presented for human verification.

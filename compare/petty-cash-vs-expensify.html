When to Choose Expensify

Expensify is the better choice if you need advanced receipt scanning with OCR, corporate card integration, mileage tracking, or if you operate internationally and need a globally-recognized expense platform. It excels at general expense management for mid-to-large companies with complex travel and expense needs.

When to Choose PettyCash

PettyCash is the better choice if petty cash is your primary need, you want denomination counting and multi-fund management, you operate in Saudi Arabia and need ZATCA compliance with native Arabic, or you want a free tool without per-user pricing. PettyCash is built for the Saudi market, not a global tool adapted to it.

FAQ

Is Expensify good for petty cash?

Expensify is excellent for general expense management and receipt scanning, but it lacks petty-cash-specific features like denomination counting, multi-fund management, and imprest system tracking.

How does pricing compare?

Expensify starts at $5/user/month. PettyCash has a free plan with no per-user pricing, making it more cost-effective for petty cash management.

Does Expensify support Arabic?

Expensify has limited Arabic support. PettyCash offers native Arabic with full RTL interface and Saudi market compliance.

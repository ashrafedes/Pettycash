Petty Cash Management for Manufacturing

Track production floor expenses, raw material petty purchases, and shift costs with real-time visibility.

The Challenge

Manufacturing floors run on small cash transactions: emergency spare parts, cleaning supplies, safety gear replacements, and worker transportation allowances. Shift supervisors keep cash on hand for these needs, but without proper tracking, costs get lumped into generic overhead with no visibility into which production line or shift is driving spending. This makes cost optimization impossible.

How PettyCash Helps

1

Shift-level tracking: Every expense is tagged to a shift, giving you per-shift cost visibility.

2

Production line allocation: Tag expenses to specific production lines for accurate cost-per-unit analysis.

3

Raw material purchases: Categorize emergency material buys separately from general supplies.

4

Safety gear tracking: Log PPE replacements and safety expenses separately for compliance reporting.

Case Study Example

A manufacturing plant with 3 production lines and 2 shifts was unable to identify which line was driving spare part costs. All petty cash was recorded under a single 'factory supplies' category. After implementing PettyCash, expenses are tagged per line and per shift. The plant manager discovered that Line 2's night shift was spending 40% more on spare parts due to older equipment, leading to a targeted maintenance investment that saved 8,000 SAR per month.

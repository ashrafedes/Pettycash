The Challenge

Clinics handle a unique mix of cash transactions: patient refunds, emergency medical supply purchases, staff reimbursements, and multi-doctor expense tracking. With patient privacy requirements and the need for precise cost allocation per department, traditional petty cash methods fall short. Paper receipts get lost, refund reasons go undocumented, and month-end reconciliation becomes a guessing game.

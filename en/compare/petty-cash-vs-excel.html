When to Choose Excel

Excel is perfectly adequate if you have a single location, one custodian, fewer than 20 petty cash transactions per month, and no need for approval workflows or real-time visibility. If you are comfortable with formulas and enjoy building your own templates, Excel gives you full control and flexibility.

When to Choose PettyCash

PettyCash is the better choice when you have multiple locations, need approval workflows, want real-time visibility across all funds, require digital receipts with audit trails, or process more than 20 petty cash transactions per month. It is also the right choice if you want to reduce reconciliation time from hours to minutes, or if you need mobile access for on-the-go expense logging.

When to Choose SAP Concur

SAP Concur is the right choice for large enterprises with thousands of employees, complex travel policies, dedicated IT and finance teams, existing SAP ERP infrastructure, and a budget for implementation consultants. If you need integrated travel booking, expense management, and invoice processing in one enterprise platform, SAP Concur delivers. But for most small and medium businesses, it is significant overkill.

When to Choose PettyCash

PettyCash is the better choice for small and medium businesses that need petty cash management without enterprise complexity. If you want to set up in minutes (not weeks), need denomination counting and multi-fund management, operate in Saudi Arabia, or simply want a free tool that does one thing well rather than an expensive platform that does everything, PettyCash is the right fit.

FAQ

Is SAP Concur overkill for small businesses?

For most small and medium businesses, SAP Concur is overkill. It is designed for enterprises with thousands of employees, complex travel policies, and dedicated finance teams.

How much does SAP Concur cost?

SAP Concur pricing is not publicly listed but typically starts at $8-12/user/month with implementation fees. PettyCash is free for petty cash management.

Does SAP Concur handle petty cash well?

SAP Concur handles expense reports and travel but does not have petty-cash-specific features like denomination counting or imprest system management.

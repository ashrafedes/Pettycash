The Challenge

NGOs face unique accountability pressures. Donors want to know exactly how their money was spent. Grants come with spending restrictions. Projects need separate budgets. And every expense must be auditable. Traditional petty cash systems with envelopes and paper receipts make it nearly impossible to produce the transparent, per-donor, per-project reports that funders demand.

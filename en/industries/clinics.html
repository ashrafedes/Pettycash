Petty Cash Management for Clinics

Handle patient refunds, medical supplies, and multi-doctor expense tracking with full audit trails.

The Challenge

Clinics handle a unique mix of cash transactions: patient refunds, emergency medical supply purchases, staff reimbursements, and multi-doctor expense tracking. With patient privacy requirements and the need for precise cost allocation per department, traditional petty cash methods fall short. Paper receipts get lost, refund reasons go undocumented, and month-end reconciliation becomes a guessing game.

How PettyCash Helps

1

Patient refunds: Log refunds with patient reference, reason, and approval — all documented for audit.

2

Medical supply tracking: Categorize emergency supply purchases separately from general expenses.

3

Per-doctor allocation: Tag expenses to specific doctors or departments for accurate cost tracking.

4

Audit-ready records: Every transaction has a digital receipt, approval trail, and category for compliance.

Case Study Example

A multi-specialty clinic with 6 doctors was struggling to allocate expenses per doctor and track patient refunds. Refunds were often paid in cash without proper documentation. After implementing PettyCash, every refund is logged with patient reference and reason, medical supply purchases are categorized per department, and the clinic administrator can see per-doctor costs in real time. Audit preparation time dropped from 2 days to 2 hours.

FAQ

Can PettyCash track expenses per doctor?

Yes, every expense can be tagged to a specific doctor or department for accurate cost allocation.

Does it handle patient refunds?

Yes, patient refunds are logged as a specific expense category with the patient reference and reason.

Is it suitable for multi-branch clinics?

Yes, each clinic branch can have its own fund with consolidated reporting across all locations.

FAQ

What denominations are included?

The template includes SAR denominations (500, 200, 100, 50, 10, 5, 1, 0.5, 0.25). You can easily modify for other currencies.

Does it calculate variance automatically?

Yes, the template includes formulas that auto-calculate the variance between counted and system amounts.

Is it printable?

Yes, the template is formatted for printing on standard A4 paper with signature lines.

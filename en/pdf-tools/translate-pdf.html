Is PDF translation free?

Yes, completely free using OpenRouter free models.

Only the extracted text is sent to the AI API. Your PDF file stays in your browser.

What languages are supported?

Over 100 languages including English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Chinese, and more.

Is the layout preserved?

The translation is provided as text output. The original layout is not preserved.

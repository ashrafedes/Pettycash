About Saudi VAT

The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia introduced Value Added Tax (VAT) on January 1, 2018, at a rate of 5%. Effective July 1, 2020, the rate was increased to 15% by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA). VAT applies to most goods and services sold or consumed in Saudi Arabia, with certain exceptions such as financial services, healthcare, and education.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the VAT rate in Saudi Arabia?

The standard VAT rate in Saudi Arabia is 15% as of July 1, 2020, set by ZATCA.

What is the difference between VAT inclusive and exclusive?

VAT exclusive means the VAT is added on top of the base amount. VAT inclusive means the VAT is already included in the stated amount.

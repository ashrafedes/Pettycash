Frequently Asked Questions

What is a budget vs actual report?

A budget vs actual report compares what you planned to spend (budget) against what you actually spent, showing variances for each category.

What is a favorable variance?

A favorable variance means you spent less than budgeted (negative variance for expenses). An unfavorable variance means you overspent.

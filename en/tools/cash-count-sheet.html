Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cash count sheet?

A cash count sheet is a document used to physically count and reconcile petty cash on hand against the expected system balance.

How often should I count petty cash?

Petty cash should be counted at least monthly, and ideally weekly or at every replenishment cycle.

What does variance mean?

Variance is the difference between the physical count and the system balance. A positive variance means you have more cash than expected; negative means less.
